---
command_id: NextForce
---

Shows the next campaign force this player controls, wrapping after the last one. It does nothing when there is no other controlled force. The command takes F9 only when that key has no binding; it can be rebound in the keyboard settings.

Switching shows that house's credits, power, production queues, and radar, and cancels pending placement and targeting modes. [Campaign mission variants](/formats/campaign-variants/#play-and-edit-a-mission) owns force switching and control assignments.
