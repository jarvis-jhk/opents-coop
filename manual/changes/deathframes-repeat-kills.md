---
title: Prevent repeated kills during vehicle death animations
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: DeathFrames
  effect: changed
credit: [ZivDero, JoyfulShush]
---

Destroyed vehicles with `DeathFrames` in their image's `art.ini` section no longer grant repeated experience or kills, or repeat Tiberium drops, when hit during their death animation.

JoyfulShush is credited for the Vinifera fix this follows.
