---
title: Firepower bonuses raise railgun beam damage
category: fix
release: 0.11.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: AmbientDamage
  effect: changed
credit: [ZivDero, Rampastring]
---

A railgun beam used to deal a positive `AmbientDamage=` to every victim without any firepower bonus. A firepower crate, the firing house's country `Firepower`, its difficulty `FirePower` and the veteran firepower bonus now raise that damage, as they raise the shot's own damage.

Rampastring is credited for the Vinifera fix this follows.
